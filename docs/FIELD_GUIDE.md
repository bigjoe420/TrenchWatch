> **Work in progress** — this guide is finalized in milestone M3 alongside

> yourself on an explorer.

## Signal inventory (v1)

1. Mint / freeze authority not revoked
2. LP tokens not burned or locked
3. Dev wallet funded within ~24 h before launch, or funded through a mixer
4. Dev wallet history: previous launches that collapsed
5. Holder concentration: top-10 holders above threshold
6. Thin liquidity: single-sided pool or TVL below floor

*Content lands with M3. Until then, treat every score as unimplemented.*
